Yoked with Him

Our Trek theme comes from the Savior’s invitation in Matthew 11:28–30: “Take my yoke upon you, and learn of me.” A yoke allows a burden to be shared and carried with greater strength. As we pull handcarts, face challenges, and help one another along the trail, we hope to better understand what it means to be yoked with Jesus Christ—to walk with Him, learn from Him, and rely upon His strength.

We Don’t Walk Alone

Trek will remind us that we were never meant to carry life’s burdens alone. Jesus Christ invites each of us to “Come unto me.” As we make and keep covenants, pray, repent, serve others, and follow Him, we become more connected to the Savior and can receive His strength, peace, and help in our lives.

A Personal Invitation

As you prepare for Trek, consider what “Yoked with Him” means to you. Where do you need the Savior’s strength? What would you like to learn from Him? Come to Trek prepared not only to walk a pioneer trail, but to draw closer to Jesus Christ. Our hope is that “Yoked with Him” will mean something more to you when Trek ends than it did when Trek began.
