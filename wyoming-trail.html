Trekking at the Wyoming Mormon Trail Sites

Walking Where the Pioneers Walked

Our 2027 Pioneer Trek will take place at the Church historic sites along the Mormon Pioneer Trail in Wyoming, including the areas near Martin’s Cove and Sixth Crossing.

These historic locations provide an opportunity to walk portions of the same landscape traveled by early pioneers and handcart companies. During Trek, we will pull handcarts, travel together as Trek families, learn about the experiences and faith of the pioneers, and spend time reflecting on our own relationship with Jesus Christ.

Trek isn't intended to simply recreate pioneer hardship. It is an opportunity to step away from everyday distractions, serve one another, strengthen relationships, and discover what it means to rely upon the Savior.

What Will Trek Be Like?

Participants will be organized into Trek families, each led by a Ma and Pa. Families will travel together, pull handcarts, eat together, participate in activities and devotionals, and help one another throughout the experience.

Days will include a combination of:

  • Handcart trekking and historic-site experiences

  • Stories and lessons from the pioneers

  • Devotionals and spiritual experiences

  • Meals and time together as Trek families

  • Opportunities for service, reflection, and journal writing

  • Plenty of hard work—and hopefully a lot of fun along the way

You don't need to be an experienced hiker or outdoors person to participate. We will provide information beforehand to help everyone prepare physically and know what clothing and equipment to bring.

Martin's Cove & Sixth Crossing

Martin's Cove and Sixth Crossing are closely connected with the experiences of the Willie and Martin handcart companies of 1856. Their stories include tremendous hardship, but they are also stories of faith, sacrifice, rescue, service, and people helping carry one another's burdens.

As we visit these places, our purpose isn't just to learn what happened there. We hope to consider what their experiences can teach us about our own lives and about turning to Jesus Christ when our burdens become difficult to carry.
